🏊 Swim FIT Repair

Drop a Garmin pool-swim export (.zip or .fit). It finds lengths the watch recorded wrong — a swim marked as rest, or a missed turn that merged two lengths into one — so you can fix them before re-uploading to Garmin Connect. Runs entirely in your browser: nothing is uploaded, nowhere.

⚠️ Delete the original activity in Garmin Connect before uploading the repaired file. Connect rejects a file it thinks it already has.
